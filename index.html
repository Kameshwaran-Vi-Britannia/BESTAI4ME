Personal AI evaluation lab

BestAI4Me

Compare answers from the AIs you use. Choose your winner blind, optionally ask Gemini for a second opinion, then use your saved rounds to track fit and Elo over time.

1 · AIs you compare

One name per line, up to 10. All six are selected by default; you can choose a smaller group for a round.

2 · Your evaluation priorities

Weights affect the personal fit score on your dashboard. They do not change Gemini's independent scores.

3 · Gemini judge (optional)

The same Gemini API key can auto-fill a Gemini answer and score the anonymized answers. The app calls Gemini only when you tap a Gemini action.

Privacy and bias: The key is saved in this browser's local storage, not in this HTML file. When you request judging or auto-fill, the relevant prompt and answers are sent to Google's Gemini API. Gemini's own answer is included in judging, so self-preference is possible. Shuffling reduces position bias but cannot eliminate it; answer length, style, and prompt wording can still affect scores. Your human pick is made before scores are shown.

4 · Backup and restore

Download a portable JSON backup before changing browsers or clearing browser data. It includes settings and all rounds (including any Gemini ratings), but never your Gemini API key. Elo is recalculated from the restored rounds.

Merge appends rounds and may duplicate rounds if you import the same file twice. Replace first downloads nothing automatically, so keep your backup file safe.

5 · Local data controls

Your ratings, preferences, rounds, and API key are stored in this browser only. Browser storage is not a substitute for a backup.
